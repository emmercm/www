## Gainful employment

In 2026, I'm happy to just be employed. I'm constantly hearing from friends that their companies are having layoffs, even multiple times in one year. I follow business news in the video game industry and it has been an awful time for creatives ([Epic Games](https://insider-gaming.com/epic-games-1000-employees-mass-layoffs/), [Bungie](https://insider-gaming.com/bungie-announces-layoffs-following-the-end-of-destiny-2/), [Xbox Game Studios and its divestments](https://insider-gaming.com/xbox-big-reset/), [id](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/report-around-half-of-the-id-software-team-have-been-laid-off), [BioWare](https://blog.bioware.com/2026/10/21/bioware-update/), Ubisoft multiple times, and so many more).

I had a lot of changes in my personal life in 2026 where I traveled or loved ones traveled to me. I'm grateful that employment allowed me to share so many special moments with so many people.

## Years of experience

Reflecting on how the software engineering industry has changed, I'm grateful to have so many years of experience before agentic coding increased the demands on everyone. I don't think technical ability, leadership, and maturity are guaranteed outcomes from years of experience, but it sure can help. I'm grateful for years of experience teaching me (often the hard way) what is good/viable/effective, bad/nonviable/ineffective, and everything in between.
