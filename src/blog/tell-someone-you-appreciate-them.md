'Tis always the season.

My current company's performance management software has a feature where you can give public or private praise to anyone in the company. An integration will post public praise to a public Slack channel so that people can join in on celebrating the recipient. I try to submit public praise through the tool as often as I can remember to.

If your HR software has a similar feature, I want to encourage you to use it. That praise might help someone get the review rating or promotion that they deserve. Maybe the praise even comes with a mini cash bonus that's completely free to you. Even if you don't have similar software, send the person a DM with a few kind words; it doesn't have to be long. Consider also sending it to their manager. People enjoy knowing that they're valued, that their care and attention is appreciated.

I truly believe that the more you do for others the more you will receive back in your life.

If you appreciate a teammate who helped you through a difficult incident, tell them. If you appreciate a teammate who had a meaningful contribution to your project, tell them. If you appreciate a colleague outside your team who was a great collaborator, tell them. If you appreciate a leader who helps you or allows you to do your best work, tell them.

Tell someone you appreciate them _today_.
