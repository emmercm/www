title: Tell Someone You Appreciate Them
tags:
- career

---

'Tis always the season.

My current company's performance management software has a feature where you can give public or private praise to anyone in the company. An integration will post public praise to a public Slack channel so that people can join in on celebrating the person. I try to submit public praise through the tool as often as I can remember to.

People enjoy knowing that they're valued, that their care and attention is appreciated. This is true both inside and outside the workplace. You don't have to use your HR software, and you don't have tell people in public; but surely there must be _someone_ who you appreciate that you haven't told recently.

If your HR software has a similar feature, I would encourage you to use it. That praise might

If you appreciate a teammate who helped you through a difficult incident, tell them. If you appreciate a teammate who had a meaningful contribution to your project, tell them. If you appreciate a colleague outside your team who was a great collaborator, tell them. If you appreciate a leader who helps you or allows you to do your best work, tell them.

Tell someone you appreciate them _today_.
