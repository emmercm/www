'Tis always the season.

My current company's performance management software has a feature where you can give public or private praise to anyone in the company. An integration will post public praise to a public Slack channel so that people can join in on celebrating the person. I try to submit public praise through the tool as often as I can remember to.

If your company has something similar, I think you should, too.

People enjoying knowing that they're valued, that their care and attention is appreciated. This is true both inside and outside the workplace.
