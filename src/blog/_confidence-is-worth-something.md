You should not ship changes that you aren't confident in. Full stop.

Years ago, I was leading a time-sensitive cross-team project and got into an argument with a senior leader. A mobile app team was code complete on a change, but had low confidence in it, and wanted time to test it. I said yes, absolutely, we should not ship changes that have a realistic chance of causing regressions. The argument I was making was that confidenceMy senior leader overruled me and had them ship the build, as the project was time-sensitive. That app version had a more than 10% crash rate on customer devices.
